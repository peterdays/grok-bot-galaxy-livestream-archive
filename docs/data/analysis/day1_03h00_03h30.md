# Day 1 — 03:00:00–03:30:00 working notes

- **03:00:00–03:02:06 — Test organic messaging before ads.** Use a real point of view that provokes discussion; paid ads are premature until the target persona and organically effective creative are known. Study top-performing posts and reuse their framing, not wording.
- **03:02:46–03:04:14 — Do not “hire great people/bots and get out of the way.”** Define outcomes, align incentives, maintain operational oversight, and track cash return, AOV, LTV, and role-specific goals. Directly applicable to agent teams.
- **03:04:24–03:06:48 — Distribution and a proof vault.** Distribution becomes a moat as software gets cheaper. Preserve screenshots, user growth, testimonials, views, permissions, and verification status from day one. Useful operating practice.
- **03:07:24–03:08:59 — Define a bot by the business outcome.** Before creating the marketing bot, ask whether the immediate goal is leads, payments, or users. With checkout/reservations absent, they choose a captured email audience and a lead magnet.
- **03:08:59–03:11:13 — “Imitate, iterate, innovate” research bot.** Reverse-engineer top local restaurateurs, their best sites/pages, and successful recent pop-ups; compile the references; ground the bot in the company brief. Strong reusable research prompt.
- **03:11:13–03:13:45 — Positioning inputs.** Ask how an expert would frame the concept and generate hooks using exclusivity, scarcity, timeliness, and relevance. Keep customer value central; overly corporate/produced creative underperforms more human material in the guest’s data.
- **03:14:43–03:18:11 — AI for traditional small businesses.** Begin with how the tool directly makes or saves money. Offer a lead magnet worth far more than the requested email, using unique data or an assessment, and connect AI capabilities to an immediate revenue stream.
- **03:18:11–03:19:28 — Guest close.** Main warning: builders overfocus on interesting products; “if you build it, they will come” is false. Compress to the distribution/revenue lesson.
