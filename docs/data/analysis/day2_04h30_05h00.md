## Most useful takeaways

- Use AI around the creative process—parallel edits, scheduling, proposals, and follow-up—while humans retain responsibility for the core idea and story.
- A useful personal assistant converts incoming information into decisions and actions. Summaries alone are less valuable than identifying a required reply, creating the calendar item, detecting a conflict, or asking for the missing judgment.
- The marketplace workflow is a clean autonomy pattern: research and execute routine steps, continue monitoring, answer from available evidence, and escalate irreversible or preference-sensitive choices such as accepting a lower price.
- Personal workflows often map directly to business workflows. Pricing used goods from comparable sales is structurally similar to vendor negotiation or sponsorship pricing.
- Cross-system retrieval reduces two costs at once: searching for context and interrupting colleagues who already know it.

## Condensed narrative

After the sales workshop ends and an eleven-minute intermission, an interview focuses on practical assistant workflows. The most concrete example is an agent that handles an item's resale lifecycle while escalating judgment calls. The broader lesson is to connect existing information systems so the agent can retrieve context and take routine actions without turning every question into a search task or a message to a colleague. There is no live coding in this half hour.
